# Trvlrr

The `trvlrr` tools read and change the signed-in person's own travel journal on
[Trvlrr](https://trvlrr.app): their trips (taken and planned) with flights, trains, stays,
activities and expenses; lifetime travel stats; their travel profile and homes; and photo
search (Trvlrr Plus). The server's own instructions describe how to use them.

Trvlrr can't book or pay for travel, check anyone in, or sign in to airline, hotel or email
accounts.
